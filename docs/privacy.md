# SpinBuddies — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

SpinBuddies is a collectible toy game. We built it to collect **no personal data**.

## What the app stores
- Your collection, coins, settings and progress are stored **only on your device**
  (app storage). They are never sent to us.
- If you use **Export save file**, the app creates a file that you choose where to keep
  or send. We never receive it.
- Thumbnails of your buddies are cached on your device to make the app faster.

## What the app does not do
- No accounts, no sign-in, no e-mail address.
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics or tracking. No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
- No real-money purchases.

## Permissions
- **Notifications** (optional): one daily reminder when your free box is ready. You can
  turn it off in Settings or in Android's settings.
- **Vibration**: for haptic feedback while spinning. Can be turned off in Settings.
- **Motion sensors**: to detect when you shake the phone to spin. Readings are used
  instantly and never stored or sent.

## Gift codes and sharing
Gift codes and share cards are created on your device. They are only shared when you
choose to send them (for example with the Android share sheet). A gift code contains
only the buddy's design code, its rarity and a random one-time number.

## Online features
The current version has no online features. If online features (friends, leaderboards)
are added later, they will use an anonymous player id and a display name you choose,
this policy will be updated before release, and they will be optional.

## Children
SpinBuddies does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Contact
Questions? Contact the developer at the e-mail address shown on the app's Play Store page.
