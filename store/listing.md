# Play Store listing — SpinBuddies

**App name (30 chars max):** SpinBuddies: Blind Box Toys

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Spin cute 3D vinyl buddies, open blind boxes, collect sets and swap with friends!

**Full description (≤ 4000 chars):**

Meet SpinBuddies — chunky, glossy 3D vinyl toys whose faces spin just like a real
face-changing figure! Swipe the face up or down (or give your phone a shake) and watch it
whirl, slow down, bounce… and land on a brand-new expression.

🎁 OPEN BLIND BOXES
Shake the box, pop the lid, and see who's inside! Every buddy has its own body, ears,
hat, outfit, accessory, colours and little 3D world. Rarities go from Common to Rare,
Epic, Legendary — and the super-rare Secret buddies with galaxy skin, glowing eyes and
halos.

📖 FILL YOUR COLLECTION BOOK
Thousands of numbered buddies to find. Complete sets like Space Crew, Hat Collector and
Rainbow to earn badges. Favourite your best finds, give them names, and recycle
duplicates for coins.

🍽️ TAKE ORDERS
Customers want very specific buddies: "sleepy face · pirate hat · snowy scene ·
something red". Lock the parts that fit and spin the rest, slot-machine style — with
only a few spins! 30 handcrafted orders on three streets, plus a new Daily Order every
day that's the same for everyone. Get three stars and you can keep the buddy you made.

🔥 DAILY GIFTS & STREAKS
A free box every day, bonus boxes on day 3 and a Premium box on day 7. Missed a day? A
streak freeze has your back once a week.

🤝 SHARE & SWAP
Make a beautiful card of any buddy and share it. Gift a buddy to a friend with a gift
code — trade by gifting each other. Works offline.

✨ MADE WITH CARE
• No ads. No real-money purchases. Coins are only earned by playing.
• No accounts, no tracking, no personal data.
• Works offline.
• Seasonal colours for Diwali, Holi, Christmas and New Year.
• All characters and sounds are original.
