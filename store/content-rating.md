# Content rating (IARC questionnaire) — notes

- Category: **Game** (casual).
- Violence: none (a foam sword and toy accessories only; no combat).
- Fear/horror: none (one "spooky" theme is cute: little horns, crescent eyes).
- Sexuality, nudity, profanity, drugs, alcohol, tobacco: none.
- Gambling: **No real-money gambling.** Blind boxes and the order "slot" spins use only
  in-game coins that cannot be bought; odds are shown in the app (Settings → Box odds).
  Answer "No" to "simulated gambling" only if Play's definition excludes random rewards
  bought with earned-only currency; otherwise answer "Yes" — this may raise the rating
  (e.g. PEGI 12 in some regions). Recommendation: answer honestly as *contains random
  item rewards (loot-box style) with in-game earned currency only*.
- User interaction: none online (offline gift codes shared outside the app).
- Shares location: no. Digital purchases: no.
- Expected result: Everyone / PEGI 3–7 (PEGI may differ due to random rewards).
- Target audience (Families policy): select **13+ / general audience** unless the owner
  wants to enrol in Designed for Families (requires extra policy review).
