# Release notes

## 0.3.2
- "Everything" spin is now a card reel: the whole scene slides away like a card and the
  new buddy's card slides in.
- Every buddy has its own voice based on its look (robots talk robot, bears talk deep…);
  your pick for a buddy is remembered.
- Fixed: turning on the daily reminder could get stuck.

## 0.3.1 — Talk & spin
- Talk to your buddy: it repeats what you say in Normal, Funny, Robot or Deep voice, with
  its head bobbing and mouth moving. Audio stays on your phone.
- "Everything" spin is now a slot-machine twirl: the old buddy spins away, the new one
  spins in, bounces, and its face rolls into place.

## 0.3.0 — Friends & Play Store prep
- Share cards: make a picture of any buddy and share it.
- Gift codes: give a buddy to a friend (works offline; trade by gifting each other).
- Accessibility: screen-reader labels everywhere, bigger small text, rarity icons + names.
- Store graphics, privacy policy and signed app bundle (AAB) builds.

## 0.2.0 — Game loop
- Coins, box shop (Blind, Premium, weekly Themed box) and duplicate recycling.
- Daily free box with streaks (day 3: 2 boxes, day 7: Premium) and a weekly streak freeze.
- Order challenge: 30 levels on 3 streets + a daily order. Lock & spin!
- 20 achievements, profile, 8 new sets, Series 2 with lots of new parts and finishes.
- Seasonal colours for Diwali, Holi, Christmas and New Year.

## 0.1.0 — Core toy
- Spinning-face 3D vinyl buddies, blind boxes with rarities, collection book with sets,
  save export/import.
