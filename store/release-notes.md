# Release notes

## 0.3.0 — Friends & Play Store prep
- Share cards: make a picture of any buddy and share it.
- Gift codes: give a buddy to a friend (works offline; trade by gifting each other).
- Accessibility: screen-reader labels everywhere, bigger small text, rarity icons + names.
- Store graphics, privacy policy and signed app bundle (AAB) builds.

## 0.2.0 — Game loop
- Coins, box shop (Blind, Premium, weekly Themed box) and duplicate recycling.
- Daily free box with streaks (day 3: 2 boxes, day 7: Premium) and a weekly streak freeze.
- Order challenge: 30 levels on 3 streets + a daily order. Lock & spin!
- 20 achievements, profile, 8 new sets, Series 2 with lots of new parts and finishes.
- Seasonal colours for Diwali, Holi, Christmas and New Year.
